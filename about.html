THE VESPER THESIS

Identity before
autonomy.

As autonomous agents interact with people, applications and blockchains, identity becomes a foundation for trust and accountability.

WHY VESPER

Agents need context.

A wallet address alone doesn't explain what an agent does, who operates it, or whether a claim about it is authentic. VESPER is exploring an identity registry that makes this context easier to find.

Start with discoverability.

Our initial concept focuses on readable profiles, capabilities and linked public wallet addresses. Later versions could add cryptographic ownership proofs, attestations and developer integrations.

Built transparently.

The current website is a front-end prototype. Example profiles are fictional, local drafts remain on your device, and verification services are not live.
